What is DASH?

DASH (Dynamic Adaptive Streaming over HTTP) is an international standard (ISO/IEC 23009-1) for streaming video over HTTP. It works by dividing video content into small segments and delivering them via a Media Presentation Description (MPD) file, an XML-based manifest that lists available resolutions, bitrates, and segment locations. The player reads the MPD, selects the best quality based on your bandwidth, and adapts dynamically as network conditions change.

How to Get MPD Links

MPD links are provided by streaming platforms, OTT services, and CDNs that implement MPEG-DASH. You can find them by inspecting network requests in your browser's developer tools (F12 → Network tab → filter by "mpd") while a DASH video is playing. Some services also expose MPD URLs in their API responses or player manifests.

Common Use Cases

  • Testing and debugging DASH streams during development
  • Verifying MPD manifest integrity and segment availability
  • Previewing adaptive bitrate behavior across network conditions
  • Validating MPEG-DASH streams before integrating into applications

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use this online DASH player?

Paste your MPD link into the input field above and click the "Play Now" button. The player uses dash.js to parse the MPD manifest and play the stream directly in your browser. No registration or software downloads required.

Does this player support DASH live streams and adaptive bitrate?

Yes. This DASH player natively supports live MPD streams, VOD content, and adaptive bitrate (ABR). It automatically selects the optimal quality for your network conditions and lets you switch quality levels manually through the player settings.

Is it safe to paste and play private DASH links on this website?

Your data privacy is completely guaranteed. This application functions 100% on the client-side. The MPD URLs you paste are parsed entirely inside your local browser and are never transmitted to any external backend server or database.

Why does my MPD link fail to play?

The most common reason is CORS (Cross-Origin Resource Sharing) restrictions. DASH servers often block direct playback from other websites. To fix this, check the "Enable Stream Proxy" option below the input field. This routes requests through our proxy server, bypassing CORS limitations. Note: We do not store or log any video content.

What is the difference between DASH and HLS (M3U8)?

Both are adaptive streaming protocols. HLS (M3U8) was created by Apple and uses a playlist format; DASH (MPD) is an ISO/IEC international standard using an XML-based manifest. DASH is codec-flexible and widely used by YouTube and Netflix, while HLS has broader native device support on iOS and Safari.
