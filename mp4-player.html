What is MP4?

MP4 (MPEG-4 Part 14) is the most widely used digital multimedia container format in the world. It can store video, audio, subtitles, and still images in a single file. Developed by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO), MP4 has become the universal standard for video distribution across the internet.

MP4 offers excellent compression efficiency while maintaining high video quality, making it ideal for streaming, downloading, and storing video content. It supports advanced codecs like H.264 (AVC) and H.265 (HEVC), enabling HD, 4K, and even 8K video playback. MP4 files are compatible with virtually all devices, browsers, and media players, which is why it remains the preferred format for platforms like YouTube, Vimeo, and social media networks.

Common use cases include online video streaming, video editing workflows, mobile video recording, surveillance systems, and video archiving. Why does my MP4 video link fail to play?

The most common reason is CORS (Cross-Origin Resource Sharing) restrictions. Video servers often block direct playback from other websites. To fix this, check the "Enable Stream Proxy" option below the input field. This routes the video through our proxy server, bypassing CORS limitations. Note: We do not store or log any video content.

While this page is optimized for MP4 playback, our player also supports M3U8/HLS live streams, WebM, and other HTML5-compatible video formats. Simply paste any video URL and the player will automatically detect the format and load the appropriate decoder.
